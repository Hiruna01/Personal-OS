## Questions 1-2

### Question 1

A student society plans a workshop. Its coordinator sets a goal, assigns tasks to volunteers, and compares attendance with the goal after the event.

Which statements are correct?

- **A.** Setting the workshop goal is planning.
- **B.** Assigning responsibilities to volunteers is organizing.
- **C.** Volunteers must receive a salary before this can be an organization.
- **D.** Comparing attendance with the goal is controlling.
- **E.** Using a shared booking platform means that the platform itself is the organization's goal.

### Question 2

Two support teams must resolve at least **80 tickets per day**. Team P resolves 100 using 50 staff-hours. Team Q resolves 90 using 60 staff-hours. Ticket difficulty and service quality are comparable.

Which conclusions are supported?

- **A.** Both teams meet the stated output target.
- **B.** Team Q is more efficient because it uses more staff-hours.
- **C.** Team P resolves 2 tickets per staff-hour, compared with 1.5 for Team Q.
- **D.** Team Q fails the target simply because Team P resolves more tickets.
- **E.** Output relative to resource use is relevant to efficiency.

## Quick answer key

| Question | Correct options |
|---|---|
| 1 | A, B, D |
| 2 | A, C, E |

## Detailed explanations

### Question 1 - Correct answers: A, B, D

- **A - Correct.** Planning establishes the goal and intended outcome before the work begins.
- **B - Correct.** Organizing assigns responsibilities and coordinates resources to carry out the plan.
- **C - Incorrect.** People can coordinate around a shared goal as volunteers. Salaries are not a requirement for an organization.
- **D - Correct.** Controlling compares actual results with a target and helps identify whether corrective action is needed.
- **E - Incorrect.** The platform is a resource used to achieve the goal; it is not the workshop's intended outcome.

### Question 2 - Correct answers: A, C, E

- **A - Correct.** Both 100 and 90 exceed the target of 80 tickets per day.
- **B - Incorrect.** More resource use does not establish greater efficiency. Compare output with the resources consumed.
- **C - Correct.** P: $100 / 50 = 2$. Q: $90 / 60 = 1.5$ tickets per staff-hour.
- **D - Incorrect.** Effectiveness is judged against the stated target. Q exceeds that target even though P resolves more tickets.
- **E - Correct.** With comparable work and quality, tickets per staff-hour provide a useful efficiency measure.

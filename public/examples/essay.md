# Essay — Integrity by design

## Prompt

Discuss the roles of application validation and relational constraints in a private academic workspace.

Consider:

- Referential integrity and ownership boundaries.
- Useful error messages versus database guarantees.
- Atomic updates and concurrent editing.

## Suggested structure

1. Introduction and thesis.
2. A concrete example of invalid data.
3. Comparison of validation layers.
4. Conclusion supported by your example.

## Self-check

Have you distinguished usability from enforcement? Include at least one example of a constraint that must remain true for every write.
